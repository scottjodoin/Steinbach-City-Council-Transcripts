CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
April 18, 2023

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
April 18, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jac Siemens, Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, and
Michael Zwaagstra. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services,
Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Jac Siemens opened the meeting.

R23-071 4. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted, as amended.

-Un. Carried-

R23-072 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the minutes of the April 4,
2023 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

R23-073 6. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve the purchase of the Elgin Pelican street sweeper as specified from Joe Johnson
Equipment for the price of $349,157.59 (plus applicable taxes).
-Un. Carried-

R23-074 7. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the following accounts be approved for payment:

$869,996.99

$ 47,479.11

$267,380.81

-Un. Carried-

R23-075 8. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the building permits as issued during the month of March 2023 be accepted. (list attached)

-Un. Carried-

accept the following Business Licences. (list attached)

Relations regarding the Mobility Disadvantaged Transportation Program for 2022 ($19,888.60.00) were acknowledged.
