CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
February 7, 2023
MINUTES
1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
February 7, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.
2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Bill Hiebert, Damian Penner, Susan Penner, Jake Hiebert and
Michael Zwaagstra. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services,
Adam Thiessen and Administrative Assistant, Amanda Dubois.
3. Councillor Bill Hiebert opened the meeting.
R23-023 4. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor S. Penner RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.
-Un. Carried-
R23-024 5. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the minutes of the
January 17, 2023 Regular Council Meeting be approved.
-Un. Carried-
R23-025 6. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that Councillor J. Siemens be excused from the meeting.
-Un. Carried-
7. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m. by Mayor
Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider By-Law 2223, Local Improvement Plan 2023-04,
Event Centre Construction.
By-Law 2223
Local Improvement Plan 2023-04
Event Centre Construction
Purpose: To authorize the borrowing of an additional $5 million for the construction of the Event Centre.
7.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced By-Law 2223, Local Improvement 2023-04 and reported that Notices pursuant to The Municipal Act had been completed.
7.2 There was no written correspondence to By-Law 2223, Local Improvement 2023-04 on file.
7.3 There was no one present at the public hearing.
7.4 There being no further discussion, the council meeting re-opened at 7:38 p.m.
R23-026 8. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor S. Penner RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give first reading to By-Law 2223 Local Improvement Plan 2023-04, Event Centre Construction.
-Un. Carried-
R23-027 9. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give second reading to By-Law 2223 Local Improvement Plan 2023-04, Event Centre Construction.
-Un. Carried-
