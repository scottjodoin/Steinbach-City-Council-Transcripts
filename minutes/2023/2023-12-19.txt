CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
December 19, 2023

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
December 5, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jac Siemens, Bill Hiebert, Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner and Michael
Zwaagstra. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services, Adam
Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Bill Hiebert opened the meeting.

R23-248 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R23-249 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the minutes of the
December 5, 2023, Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

R23-250 6. Councillor J. Siemens, Council B. Hiebert RESOLVED that Councillor D. Penner be excused from the meeting.

-Un. Carried-

7. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2023-22.

Variance V-2023-22

Owners: [names withheld]

Applicant: [name withheld]

Civic Address: 317 PTH 52 West

Legal: Lot 1, Plan 48097

Purpose: To permit an east side yard setback of four feet for an accessory building whereas the “DR1” Development Reserve 1 Zone requires a fifteen foot side yard Setback

7.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Variance V-2023-22 and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

7.2 There being no further discussion Council meeting re-opened at 7:37 p.m.

R23-251 8. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach deny
Variance V-2023-22.
Voting In Favor: J. Hiebert, J. Siemens, E. Funk, S. Penner, M. Zwaagstra
Voting Against: B. Hiebert

9. Troy Warkentin, City Manager presented an Event Centre Update

9.1 Reviewed the timeline regarding excavation, ground breaking, piling and grade beam work, as well as the Centennial Arena demolition.

9.2 Masonry work is currently approximately 50% complete, with a completion date of two to three months. Steel structure and framing to start as early as January 2024.

9.3 Progress report reviewed, with the next update March 2024.
