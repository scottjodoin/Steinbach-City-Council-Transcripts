CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
October 17, 2023

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
October 17, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jac Siemens, Bill Hiebert, Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Susan
Penner and Michael Zwaagstra. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Damian Penner opened the meeting.

R23-205 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R23-206 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the minutes of the October
3, 2023 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

$ 51,312.80

$ 305,384.80

R23-208 7. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor D. Penner, RESOLVED that the Financial

-Un. Carried-

R23-209 8. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the building permits as issued during the month of September 2023 be accepted. (list attached)

R23-211 10. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give first reading to By-Law 2233, to replace Residential Capital Development Fee By-Law 2217,
Water & Sewer Impact Fee By-Law 1883 & Non-Residential Capital Development Fee By-Law
2128 with an increase of rates of 5% per year for the next four years.

Voting In Favor: M. Zwaagstra, S. Penner,
E. Funk, B. Hiebert & J. Siemens

Voting Against: D. Penner & J. Hiebert

R23-212 11. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert

WHEREAS Section 35(1) of The Municipal Assessment Act provides authority that a council shall each year by resolution appoint a board of revision consisting of not less than three members;

AND WHEREAS one of the appointed members of the board shall serve as the presiding

officer of the board;

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the following persons be appointed to the 2024

board of revision scheduled to be held on October 24, 2023, with the designated role, and the

amount of honorarium, as follows:

Matt Wieler

Presiding Officer

$300.00

Jack Kehler

Member

$250.00

Roy Enns

Member

$250.00

-Un. Carried-

R23-213 12. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach award the Lift Station #2 Improvements project to Penn-co Construction Canada (2003) Ltd. in the amount of $1,996,642.57, and that additional funding from the City’s Utility Reserve be authorized in the amount of $250,000.00.

-Un. Carried-

R23-214 13. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach re- appoint Reid & Miller Chartered Professional Accountants Inc. as the City Auditor for a one- year term (2023) in the estimated amount of $57,500.00 (plus applicable taxes).

-Un. Carried-

14. Correspondence and a 2023 final grant payment ($811,283.35) from the Minister of
Municipal Relations regarding the 2023 Municipal Operating Grant were acknowledged.

Received as information.

15. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the meeting be adjourned.

-Un. Carried-

Time of adjournment: 8:00 p.m.
