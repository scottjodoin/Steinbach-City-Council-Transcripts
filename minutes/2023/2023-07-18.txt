CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
July 18, 2023

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
July 18, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jac Siemens, Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner and Michael Zwaagstra.
Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and
City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Susan Penner opened the meeting.

9. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2023-10.

Variance V-2023-10

Owner: Sommerfeld Mennonite Church of Manitoba

Applicant: Loewen Henderson Banman Legault LLP.

Civic Address: 420 Hespeler Street South

Legal: Lot 54, Plan 11504

Purpose: 1.
To permit an overall site area of 5.22 acres whereas the “DR1”
Development Reserve 1 Zone requires a minimum site area of 20 acres;
2. To Permit an overall site width of 264 feet whereas “DR1” Development
Reserve 1 Zone requires a minimum site width of 600 feet.

9.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Variance V-2023-10 and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

9.2 Ernest Schroeder, on behalf of the Sommerfeld Mennonite Church of Manitoba, was prepared to answer questions of Council.

9.3 There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:44 p.m.

R23-149 10. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2023-10, subject to an approved lot grading plan being submitted and accepted by the City.

-Un. Carried-

11. Delegation: Christine Beaumont, President of the Steinbach Chamber of Commerce, 284
Reimer Avenue, was present at the meeting.

11.1 Ms. Beaumont highlighted the growth of the organization including Chamber of
Commerce Staff, Eastman Immigrant Services, and the Steinbach Economic Development Corp.
A review of current office space and the possibility of relocating to accommodate all three in the same space is being conducted.

11.2 Leading growth and connecting people continues to be the vision of the Chamber.
Advocating on behalf of Steinbach and the business community, running Eastman Immigrant
Services and the Steinbach Economic Development Corp, and participating at the Provincial level.

11.3 Ms. Beaumont confirmed that the Chamber would be hosting Premier Heather Stefanson in August 2023.

11.4 The Chamber focus also includes bringing people together by hosting events such as the
Taste of Steinbach, Burger Days, Mixers, Round Table Mentor Events

11.5 Ms. Beaumont highlighted the Employment Facilitation program thru Eastman
Immigrant Services which connects employers with newcomers.

11.6 Mayor Funk thanked Ms. Beaumont and the Chamber of Commerce for their presentation and for their support of the residents of Steinbach.

12. Delegation: Michelle Bezditny, Director of Economic Development, D4-284 Reimer
Avenue, was present at the meeting.

12.1 Ms. Bezditny described the significant progress made in the establishment of incorporation, defining their area of focus while leveraging Steinbach competitive advantage, strengths, and opportunities.

12.2 Ms. Bezditny outlined the asset evaluation project done in March-May 2023. She outlined what data was collected, how information was compiled and what they learned. They were able to identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.

12.3 The Chamber’s goal is to transition from an agriculture business attraction as a primary strategy to agribusiness and make Steinbach the center of the advanced agribusiness on the
Canadian prairies. To make economic development successful a collaboration between the
Economic Development Corporation, the City of Steinbach, the region and beyond is required.

12.4 Mayor Funk thanked Ms. Bezditny and the Economic Development Corporation for their presentation and is looking forward to how their vision is developed in the future.

13. Ms. Beaumont, President of the Steinbach Chamber of Commerce, returned to the podium to present the new “More for You Here” Chamber of Commerce video.
