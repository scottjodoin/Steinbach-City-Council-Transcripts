CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
January 17, 2023

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
January 17, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Bill Hiebert, Jac Siemens, Damian Penner, Susan Penner, and
Michael Zwaagstra. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services,
Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Deb Rempel.

3. Councillor Michael Zwaagstra opened the meeting.

R23-012 4. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted as amended.

-Un. Carried-

3, 2023 Regular Council Meeting be approved.
