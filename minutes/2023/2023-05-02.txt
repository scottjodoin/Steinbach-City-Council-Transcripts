CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
May 2, 2023

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
May 2, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jac Siemens, Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, and
Michael Zwaagstra. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services,
Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Michael Zwaagstra opened the meeting.

R23-077 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R23-078 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the minutes of the April 18,
2023 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Delegation: RCMP Sargent, Guy Landreville, was present at the meeting.

6.1 Staff Sargent Landreville outlined and elaborated on the Council policing priorities and performance for 2023-2024

a. Communication - Visibility of Police

b. Crime Reduction - Organized Crime

c. Crime Reduction - Property Crimes/Break and Enters/Vandalism

d. Crime Reduction - Traffic

6.2 Staff Sargent Landreville reviewed recent street racing incidents that resulted in fines, vehicle impoundment and license reviews by Manitoba Public Insurance.

6.3 5 provincial traffic members will now be focusing on the Southeast corner of the perimeter and will be participating in traffic initiatives within the Steinbach area.

6.4 Staff Sargent Landreville urged people to call in and report incidents as they witness them. Calls can be reported anonymously through Crime Stoppers, thru the Steinbach dispatch number, or by calling 911 if lives are at risk.

6.5 Staff Sargent Landreville was prepared to answer questions of council.

6.6 Mayor Funk thanked the Staff Sargent for his report and for working together with council to provide a safe community.

R23-079 7. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the policing priorities be approved as outlined.

-Un. Carried-

R23-080 8. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve the purchase of the E-One Ladder Truck through the AMM sponsored Sourcewell procurement program for $2,135,475.14 including delivery (plus applicable taxes).

10. Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided a report on the PTH 12 North traffic Signals
Coordination.

January 3, 2023 a request by council for administration to discuss concerns of the traffic light signalization a lot PTH 12 North with Manitoba Transportation and Infrastructure.

After speaking with the regional manager of Manitoba Transportation and Infrastructure,
Ryan Coulter it was determined:

A review of all interactions along PTH 12 North has been completed. Traffic signal coordination and adjustments based on the departments review, several intersections were determined to have programming cycles requiring correction.

Further review determined that some of the technology used on certain intersections would benefit from upgrading and/or replacement. No timeline has been identified.

Many concerns observed by the public, should now be resolved with noticeable improvements.

Additional or ongoing concerns are encouraged to be addressed by contacting the local
Manitoba Transportation and Infrastructure office.

18. Correspondence from the Steinbach Pistons regarding a request for the rink glass donation were acknowledged.
