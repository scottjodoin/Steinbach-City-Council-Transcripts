CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
November 7, 2023

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
November 7, 2023 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jac Siemens, Bill Hiebert, Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Susan
Penner and Michael Zwaagstra. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Susan Penner opened the meeting.

Voting In Favor: M. Zwaagstra, S. Penner, E. Funk, B. Hiebert & J. Siemens

Voting Against: D. Penner & J. Hiebert

9. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:46 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Public Lane Closing By-law 2232.

By-Law 2232 – Public Lane Closing

Purpose: To close all that portion of Public Lane Plan 8654 WLTO.

9.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced rezoning By-Law 2232, reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

9.2 One written objection was submitted.

9.3 There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.

9.4 There being no further discussion Council meeting re-opened at 7:55 p.m.

14. Councillor J. Siemens provided comments regarding traffic concerns at Hespeler Street and Loewen Boulevard, including difficulty making left hand turns off of Hespeler onto Loewen
Boulevard, and traffic flow backups at peak traffic periods throughout the day.

14.1 Consensus of council was for administration have a traffic count analysis completed for that intersection, compare and provide results of the studies done from 2016-2019 and present the report, and a recommendation to a future council meeting.

15. Minutes of the Seine Rat Roseau Watershed District of September 19, 2023 and
October 6, 2023 were acknowledged.
