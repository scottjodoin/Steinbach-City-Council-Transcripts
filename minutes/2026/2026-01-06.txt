CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
January 6, 2026

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
January 6, 2026, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Damian Penner,
Jac Siemens & Bill Hiebert. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Senior Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Mayor Earl Funk opened the meeting.

R26-001 4. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted as amended.

Remove: Item 9C Zoning By-Law 2245 (3rd reading)

Add: Item 12A Proposed Operating Agreement between Steinbach Source for Sports and the Southeast Events Group Inc.

-Un. Carried-

R26-002 5. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the minutes of
December 16, 2025 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:43 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Conditional Use CU-2025-06 and
Variance V-2025-38.

Conditional Use CU-2025-06 & Variance V-2025-38

Owner: LT Neufeld Holdings Ltd.
Applicant: Three Way Builders Ltd.

Civic Address: 31 Loewen Boulevard

Legal: Desc. Part of Lots 8 & 9, Plan 5634

Purpose of Conditional Use CU-2025-06: To allow a mixed-use commercial/multi-family development within the “C3” Commercial Corridor Zone.

Purpose of Variance V-2025-38: To allow a west side yard setback of five feet whereas the minimum setback required in the “C3” Commercial Corridor Zone is fifteen feet.

6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Conditional Use CU-2025-06 and Variance
V-2025-38, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

6.2 Nic Thiessen, 50 Granite Park Boulevard, on behalf of the applicant, Three Way Builders
Ltd. Was present at the hearing and provided the following information:

-
The development will include 33 residential units on floors 2-4 and over 10,000 sq.ft. of commercial space on the main floor.
-
There is a lot of support for mixed use development, which combines commercial space with multi-family residences bringing vibrance to the community preventing empty revenue, enhancing local business while creating safer, more welcoming streetscapes.
Residents benefit from nearby amenities, reduced traffic and less reliance on cars.
-
The neighbourhood already includes comparable mixed-use buildings on highway 52.

6.3 There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.

6.4 There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:49 p.m.

R26-003 7. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Conditional Use CU-2025-06, subject to the execution of a development agreement.

-Un. Carried-

8. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:51 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2025-38.

9. Nic Thiessen, 50 Granite Park Boulevard, on behalf of the applicant, Three Way Builders
Ltd. Was present at the hearing and provided the following information:

-
Given the location and other existing structure, the request is reasonable. Many of the city’s residential zonings only require four- or five-foot side yard setbacks and most commercial zones have a zero-foot side yard requirement.
-
Other properties nearby such as 37 Loewen Boulevard, 77 PTH 52 West and 2 PTH
12 North all have the same zoning as 31 Loewen Boulevard and have reduced side yards.
-
There will be no negative impact to the neighbourhood.

10. There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.

11. There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:51 p.m.

R26-004 12. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2025-38, subject to the execution of a development agreement.

-Un. Carried-
