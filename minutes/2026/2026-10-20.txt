1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
October 20, 2026, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:28 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Damian Penner, Jac Siemens, Bill
Hiebert & Jake Hiebert. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Director of Corporate
Services, Adam Thiessen, City Planner, Lacey Gaudet and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

-Un. Carried-

R26-207 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the minutes of

-Un. Carried-

6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:34 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2026-21.

Variance V-2026-21

Owner: LT Neufeld Holdings Ltd.

Applicant: [name withheld]

Civic Address: 31 Loewen Boulevard
Legal: Desc Part of Lots 8 & 9 Plan 6534

Purpose of Variance V-2026-21: To reduce the required commercial component on the main floor of a mixed-used building within the “C3” Commercial Corridor Zone. The applicant is proposing to provide one commercial tenant space on the main floor, with the remainder of the main floor consisting of residential dwelling units.

6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Variance V-2026-21, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, provided that no items of correspondence had been received.

7. Nic Thiessen, 50 Granite Park Boulevard, representing the applicant, was present at the public hearing and provided the following:

- They have struggled to find commercial tenants to lease the main space. They have found there is an excess of capacity within Steinbach for commercial space or that most commercial tenants that would be looking for the size of spaces offered do not want to wait the twelve to fourteen months it will take to have the building completed.

- One tenant has signed a pre-lease, which will be located at the front of the building, facing the street on Loewen Boulevard, which is between 18,000 or 19,000 sq.ft.

- Six additional residential suites would be developed in the remaining main floor space.

8. There being no further discussion Council meeting re-opened at 7:38 p.m.

R26-208 9. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2026-21 to reduce the required commercial component on the main floor of a mixed-use building within the “C3” Commercial Corridor Zone.

-Un. Carried-
