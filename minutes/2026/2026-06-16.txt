15. Roger Storoschuk, Assessor, Manitoba Municipal Relations - Assessment Services attended the meeting as a delegation. He provided information on the overview of the 2027 re-assessment.
- Steinbach’s overall increase was approximately nine percent.
- He provided details on how taxable assessments have changed in the City and across the province.
- The objectives of the province wide re-assessment are equity – to ensure that assessments keep pace with the changing market conditions; fairness – assessments remain current to ensure everyone pays their fair share of taxes; greater ratepayer understanding – updating property assessments make it easier for property owners to understand and evaluate their property.
- He provided information on changes in the assessment and that the reassessments reflect how property values have changed.
- He provided information on the impact of the reassessment for 2027 and that the average single family residential property will see an increase of 3.0 percent, average apartment unit an increase of 3.1 percent, and average condominium an decrease of 0.2 percent.

- Property owners will receive notification of the 2027 reassessment this week. Residents can see their reassessment online at MyPropertyMB. Additional information can be found on the
Provincial website or by contacting the Steinbach assessment office to speak with an assessor.

15.1 The Delegate was prepared to answer questions of Council.

16. Mayor Funk thanked the delegate for the presentation and information provided on the
2027 reassessment.

R26-129 22. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor D. Penner

WHEREAS the City of Steinbach submitted its DFA applications in good faith, with the expectation that the Province would give full and fair consideration to the circumstances and impacts experienced by Steinbach residents;

AND WHEREAS the Disaster Financial Assistance (DFA) program denied the City of
Steinbach's requests for assistance related to the significant rainfall events that residents of
Steinbach experienced substantial property damage and financial hardship as a result of the 2024 and 2025 rainfall events;

AND WHEREAS Premier Wab Kinew has publicly stated that his administration is
“looking at ensuring this DFA program is going to be there for people so that no one in rural
Manitoba gets left behind, and we’re not going to let the rules, as posting on the website, stand in the way of doing the right thing.”;

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach formally request that the
Province of Manitoba reconsider, reopen and approve the City of Steinbach’s Disaster Financial
Assistance application related to the 2024 and 2025 rainfall events in Steinbach.
