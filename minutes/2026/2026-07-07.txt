6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:36 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2026-13.

Variance V-2026-13

Owners: 10261601 MB Ltd. & 10261598 MB Ltd.

Civic Address: 80 & 81 Lund Road

Legal: Lot 2 Plan 46706 & all the portions of Lot 33 Plan 4068 lying to the West of the
Western limit of Giesbrecht Street Plan 5054 which lies to the East of the Eastern limit of
Lund Road Plan 14676 Excl all those portions contained within the limits of Plan 14811 &
14953.

Purpose of Variance V-2026-13: To allow a maximum height of three stories for a multi-family development whereas Zoning By-Law Amendment 2015 limited the height to a maximum of two stories.

6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Variance V-2026-13, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided that no items of correspondence had been received.

6.3 Stefan Hodelmann, 454 Hanover Street, representing applicant [name withheld], was present at the public hearing and presented the following:

- The larger parcel shown has the restriction of two stories. Both properties are currently zoned
RMD. Thirteen years ago a public hearing occurred and the restriction of two storeys was placed on the property.
- Multi-family development has changed since the restriction was put in place. The developer would like to have consistency between the two properties, allowing for consistent design.
- This application is strictly regarding height and not density. The same density can be achieved by utilizing underground parking and a two-storey design. The applicant prefers to go with three storeys and not two to build affordable rental units instead of luxury units.
- A dedicated building with three storeys would allow for more green space and additional amenities.
- The developer has not worked through the design process to determine the design of the building until a decision is made on whether three stories would be permitted.

7. [Resident 1], 42 Westview Drive was present at the hearing with questions regarding what the height of the structure would be, and once confirmed, residents will be presented with the plan before it is approved. She also asked if Lund Road would be opened all the way to PTH Hwy
52 or will all the traffic come into their neighborhood.

--- Page 2 ---

8. Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided that the conceptual plan approved for the neighbourhood envision access from the two subject properties would be gained south from Ellice
Avenue. Lund Road would not be open to the North to prevent residential and industrial uses being mixed. In the original approval for the rezoning to RMD the intention was not to carry that road through as there are two access points to the subject neighbourhood. One by Ellice Avenue to Giesbrecht Street and the other one further south from Wood Haven Avenue to Giesbrecht
Street.

9. [Resident 1], 42 Westview Drive, voiced concerns regarding the amount of children playing in the area and how much increased traffic flow they could see. How will that change the traffic flow coming through because there is a park nearby and many children ride their bikes and play in the street.

10. [Resident 2], 35 Westview Drive, was present at the hearing. The proposed building on the larger property will be immediately in their backyard. He cited traffic concerns, drainage issues, concern for water and sewage usage, reduced property value and loss of privacy. He asked if they would be required to install a fence?

11. Mr. Hodelmann returned to the podium and explained that most typical three-storey buildings are between thirty and thirty-two feet and stressed that the design of the building has not been developed yet. Conceptual plans indicate approximately 240 units. Drainage will be addressed through a development agreement and will have to meet design and engineering standards. Privacy concerns can be addressed with balconies not facing backyards, but they have not reached that stage of design yet. There will be a fence, and there is a forty-five foot existing buffer.

12. Troy Warkentin, City Manager, confirmed that Ellice Avenue is classified as a local street and Giesbrecht Street is classified as a collector street, which is intended to carry higher volumes of traffic.

13. There being no further discussion Council meeting re-opened at 7:58p.m.

R26-132 14. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2026-13, subject to the execution of a development agreement.

-Un. Carried-
