1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
February 3, 2026, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Damian Penner,
Jac Siemens & Bill Hiebert. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Senior Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Michael Zwaagstra opened the meeting.

R26-021 4. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R26-022 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the minutes of
January 20, 2026 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:37 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Conditional Use CU-2026-01.

Conditional Use CU-2026-01

Owners: 6831576 Manitoba Ltd. & Reimer Agencies Ltd.

Applicant: Waldo Neustaedter

Civic Address: 119 & 123 Industrial Road

Legal: Lots 2 & 3, Plan 54128

Purpose: To allow a social services facility to be located on the subject parcels within the
“M1” Light Industrial Zone.

6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager introduced Conditional Use CU-2026-01 and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

6.2 The applicant, Waldo Neustaedter, 101-32 Brandt Street, was present at the public hearing and presented the following information:

- He first met Trailblazers in 2017
- They have experienced growth and have several locations in Steinbach.
- They have day programs and transport over 100 people, as well as employing approximately 90 individuals.
- They feel this location is ideal for them because of visibility, accessibility and affordability.
- If they are permitted to build in this location, their plan is for a 5,000 sq.ft. building with the capability of adding a future 7,000 sq.ft. building behind it.
- They would provide required parking for both buildings.

6.3 Mr. Neustaedter was prepared to answer questions of Council.

6.4 Councillor B. Hiebert inquired anticipated timeline for construction of the second building.

6.5 Councillor D. Penner asked whether all operations would be consolidated at the new location.

--- Page 2 ---

6.6 Mr. Neustaedter responded that construction of the second building is anticipated within two to three years. He noted that not all operations would be relocated to the new site. The transportation division will remain at its existing location on PTH 12, while day programming and corporate offices will move to the new location.
