CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
January 20, 2026

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
January 20, 2026, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Damian Penner,
Jac Siemens & Bill Hiebert. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Senior Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Susan Penner opened the meeting.

R26-007 4. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R26-008 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor S. Penner RESOLVED that the minutes of
January 6, 2026 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided the following information regarding Library
Regionalization within Southeast Manitoba:

-
This matter has been brought before Council in response to a 2024 proposal from Public
Library Services to investigate the feasibility of establishing a regional library in Southeastern
Manitoba.
-
Council approved the appointment of two members of Council to participate on the working group. The working group has now completed its work, which has resulted in the attached report.
-
The report provides recommendations and supporting information for Council’s consideration in determining whether to proceed with regionalization.
-
Information from the Jake Epp Library Board has not been made available for inclusion in this report.

6.1 Mr. Warkentin was prepared to answer questions of council.

R26-009 7. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor B. Hiebert

RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach end its investigation into the library regionalization process as currently proposed by Public Library Services;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Jake Epp Library continues to operate with its current governance and service model.

-Un. Carried-

8. Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided the following information regarding the proposed purchase of land at 148 Hanover Road East:

-
The subject property has been listed for sale for approximately two years.
-
Administration has determined it would be appropriate to make an offer on the subject property.
-
There is no immediate requirement to incorporate the property into the City’s landfill operations.
-
In the interim, the property could provide opportunities for administrative and general use until such time as the land is required for landfill-related services.

8.1 Mr. Warkentin was prepared to answer questions of council.
