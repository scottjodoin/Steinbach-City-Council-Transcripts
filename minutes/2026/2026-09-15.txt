CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
September 15, 2026

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
September 15, 2026, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Damian Penner, Jac Siemens, Bill
Hiebert & Jake Hiebert. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Director of Corporate
Services, Adam Thiessen, and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor B. Hiebert opened the meeting.

R26-190 4. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor S. Penner RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R26-191 5. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the minutes of
September 1, 2026 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Delegation: RCMP Staff Sergeant Maxwell was present at the meeting.

6.1 Staff Sergeant Maxwell reviewed Steinbach priorities and reported that both the municipal and rural components at full complement with a total of 18 municipal members which included 1
Staff Sergeant, 3 corporals, 2 general investigation section investigators, 1 traffic services investigator, 11 General duty Constables.

6.2 Staff Sergeant Maxwell spoke to Police visibility in the community.

-
The return of the Traffic Services member to full duties is expected to significantly increase police visibility, particularly in Steinbach.
-
The Traffic Services member is dedicated to municipal policing and spends the majority of their time working within the City.
-
Special Constable Dennis Redekop provides an additional visible police presence through work in local schools.
-
RCMP visibility is also increased through media releases, local media outlets and social media.

6.3 Calls for services have remained relatively consistent, with 4,700 to date, while rural calls for service have decreased. There has been a significant increase in mental health act calls, at 311 for 2026, which represents more than one mental health related call per day. He noted that RCMP members spend considerable time working with partner agencies on mental health crises.

While well-being checks have increased substantially, he cautioned that the statistics can be misleading because multiple calls may involve the same individuals over the course of a year.

6.4 Break and enters have increased from 78 in 2025 to 91 in 2026, while arsons have decreased significantly with 11 in 2025 to 2 in 2026.

6.5 Staff Sergeant Maxwell reviewed the Crime Severity Index, which considers the severity of offences, and confirmed that Steinbach was similar to the Canadian average and lower than the
Manitoba average.

6.6 The dedicated Traffic Service position is important as traffic enforcement in areas such as
Main Street remains a concern. He explained that they had recently conducted a targeted traffic enforcement project on Main Street, with additional targeted enforcement planned. He intended to utilize designated resources and planned operations rather than relying on officers to conduct traffic enforcement between calls for service.

6.7 Staff Sergeant Maxwell was prepared to answer questions of council.

7. Councillor M. Zwaagstra commended the Staff Seargent on his willingness to dive in and has been impressed with his leadership. He asked him to share any challenges he has experienced with catching offenders on Main Street.

7.1 Staff Sargeant Maxwell explained that it can be difficult for members to dedicate time for proactive patrols as they are kept busy and must triage the work that they do. A dedicated resource is important to the success of the task. Moving forward they will continue to have targeted plans with dedicated resources.

8. Councillor J. Siemens welcomed the Staff Sargeant to Steinbach and asked if the undercover
GIS officer hired to address racing along Main Street 6-8 years ago was still active in Steinbach as well as his thoughts on E-Bikes and E-Scooters.

8.1 He confirmed that the Steinbach detachment has two plain clothes GIS positions, which conduct detailed investigation and can be used strategically to support enforcement initiatives, including the most recent traffic enforcement initiative.

He noted that legislation and enforcement strategies have not yet caught up with the technology of
E-vehicles. Safety concerns regarding speed and injuries are increasing. Once legislation and regulations are put in place, he expects that the RCMP and CSO’s will have a role in enforcement once appropriate rules are established.

9. Councillor J. Hiebert asked about the use of officers from other detachments and if the
City is paying the overtime when members are brought in.

9.1 The Staff Sergeant explained that overtime is typically charged according to the position being replaced rather than where the officer is assigned. As municipal and rural members work together as one detachment, overtime is not divided according to where individual calls for service occur.

10. Mayor Funk thanked the Staff Sergeant for his leadership in the community.

11. Delegation: Madeline Wieler and Tammy Beauregard of STARS present at the meeting and presented the following fiscal year in review:

- 1,181 missions were flown in Manitoba, 47 calls within the City of Steinbach and approximately
47 calls in the surrounding area, with Steinbach being one of the top three destinations for service.
- In the last 14 years STARS has flown over 9500 missions, launched a mobile education unit to train rural health practitioners, implemented world leading mass hemorrhage protocols, and obtained new state of the art Airbus H145 helicopters.
- They have become a leader in equipping and training teams with pre-hospital ultrasound and other innovative diagnostic tools, enabled aircraft to carry pediatric patients and partnered with communities to set up fuel caches which extend their range across the province.
- They have responded to 472 recreational vehicles incidents, cared for 209 patients with 370 units of blood and cared for 223 pediatric patients.
- For the past 14 years STARS has operated out of Manitoba they have worked out of aqua trailers.
By the end of the year, the expansion should be complete. There will be education labs and a sim room.
- An invitation to council was extended to visit and witness the impact of STARS and what they are able to accomplish.

11.1 Ms. Wieler was prepared to answer questions of council.

12. Mayor Funk thanked Ms. Wieler and Ms. Beauregard and acknowledged that they don’t just impact one life at a time, but the entire family of the person utilizing their services.

13. Councillor J. Hiebert questioned with 472 recreational vehicle incidents if they saw a rise in accidents with E-vehicles.

13.1 Ms. Wieler provided that with rural living they see a lot of recreation vehicle accidents in general and recommended wearing a helmet.

14. Councillor B. Hiebert asked about the possibility of a helipad in Steinbach.
