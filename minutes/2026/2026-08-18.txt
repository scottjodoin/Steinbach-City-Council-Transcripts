6. David Paton, Regional Professional Land Use Planner and Local City Liaison for WSP attended the meeting as a delegation. He provided the following information on the City of
Steinbach Climate Risk Assessment and Adaptation Plan. The City of Steinbach developed a climate risk assessment and adaptation plan to proactively address extreme impacts on municipal infrastructure, services and the community. The plan identifies extreme weather hazards, vulnerabilities and adaptation actions to improve resilience across time horizons.

6.1 Steinbach has already experienced impacts from extreme weather. Future risks include intense rainfall, extreme heat, drought, wildfire smoke, severe storms and increasing frequency of freeze-thaw cycles. These events can affect roads, drainage systems, buildings, parks, services and public safety.

6.2 The purpose of the plan is to provide a practical roadmap to reduce risks to infrastructure, maintain reliable municipal services, improve emergency preparedness, support long term asset management and increase community resilience.

6.3 Mr. Paton stressed that this was a planning document, not a commitment to implement every action immediately.

6.4 Service areas were split into four categories, identified the following high priority risks and recommended actions:

Municipal Services: drainage, stormwater, water systems and emergency response
-Stormwater planning studies, enhanced drainage inspections and emergency preparedness updates.

Transportation: road flooding, winter maintenance and pavement performance
-Improve drainage capacity, evaluate heat-resistant paving and update winter maintenance.

Buildings: Back up power, heat management and indoor air quality
-Continue backup power improvements, HVAC and cooling upgrades when appropriate and improve roof inspections.

Parks and Recreation: Tree health, drought impacts, flooding and erosion.
-Tree canopy development, drought tolerant landscaping, resilient trails and public spaces.

6.5 The implementation approach is flexible and phased. Actions can be phased based on costs, resource availability and allocation.

6.6 Mr. Paton was prepared to answer questions of council.
