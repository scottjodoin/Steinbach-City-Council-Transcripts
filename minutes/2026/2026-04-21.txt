18. Troy Warkentin, City Manager, provided the following regarding the Manning Canal
Memorandum of Understanding:
-
One of the drains within the City is the Manning Canal, which is designated a provincial waterway and enters city limits at its southeastern boundary at the intersection of Hanover Road
East and Hirschfeld Road.
-
Since 2022, ongoing negotiations between the City and Manitoba Transportation and
Infrastructure has occurred in which the City of Steinbach has requested consideration from the province to have jurisdiction and authority transferred to the city over this particular drain.
-
It is the only drain that runs through Steinbach that is still under full provincial jurisdiction.
-
The authorization to enter into this memorandum of understanding agreement is the appropriate first step as the formal transfer of jurisdiction from the Province of Manitoba to the city requires a lieutenant Governor in Council decision can proceed.

R26-081 19. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach enter into a Memorandum of Understanding respecting the portions of the Manning Canal located within
City limits, in order to facilitate the official transfer of jurisdiction from the Province of Manitoba to the City of Steinbach.

-Un. Carried-

20. Adam Thiesen, Senior Manager, Corporate Services, provided the following regarding the
Disaster Financial Assistance Request for the September 11, 2025 Rain Event:
-
Specific criteria for several of the categories that the Emergency Management Division is not available for when they assess a community for the DFA program.
-
Manitoba EMO utilizes the Federal DFAA Program guidelines and criteria to assess insurability. The Federal DFAA Program advises Provincial EMO’s to assess regional insurability as a first and primary criteria for opening a claim above all other criteria and does not factor in repeated events.
-
They contact insurance companies locally and within the region and determine if the City is insurable for the event, rather than at the individual or property level.
