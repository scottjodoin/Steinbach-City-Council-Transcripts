CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
November 15, 2022

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
November 15, 2022 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Bill Hiebert, Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Jac Siemens, Michael
Zwaagstra and Susan Penner. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate
Services, Adam Thiessen, and City Clerk, Deb Rempel.

3. Councillor Jake Hiebert opened the meeting.

R22-259 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R22-260 5. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the minutes of the
November 1, 2022 Inaugural Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

R22-261 6. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the minutes of the
November 1, 2022 Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

7. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m., by Mayor
Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Conditional Use CU-2022-04.

Conditional Use CU-2022-04

Owner: 10134593 Manitoba Ltd.

Applicant: ROC Eastman Inc.

Lot 2 Plan 64190

Unit 9 & 10 - 110 Industrial Road

Purpose: To allow the operation of a retail sales business in Units 9 & 10 110 Industrial
Road on the subject property.

7.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager introduced Conditional Use CU-2022-04 and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

7.2 There are no items of correspondence are on file.

7.3 Stefan Hodelmann, the applicant on behalf of 10121375 Manitoba Ltd. was present at the hearing and provided information on the application. He asked Council’s consideration to consider approving only Units 9 & 10 - 110 Industrial Road to allow a retail space. The applicant provided that there will be limited retail use at the location, as this space will be used for storage of books for the majority of the time. ROC Eastman Inc. (Recreation Opportunities for Children) has indicated the organization will offer book sales 4 times a year.

The applicant was prepared to answer questions of Council.

7.4 Council asked questions of the applicant. Questions also asked of administration and about how conditions could be enforced on a Conditional Use approval.

7.5 There being no further discussion Council meeting re opened at 7:52 p.m.

R22-262 8. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Conditional Use CU-2022-04, subject to the execution of a major occupancy agreement; subject to the approval being applicable only to Unit 9 and Unit 10 of 110 Industrial Road, Lot 2,
Plan 64190, and subject to the approval be for ROC Eastman Inc. (Recreation Opportunities for
Children) only.

-Carried-
Voting in Favor: M. Zwaagstra, J. Siemens. S. Penner, B. Hiebert, E. Funk
