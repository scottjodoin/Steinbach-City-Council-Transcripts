CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
December 6, 2022
MINUTES
1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
December 6, 2022 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.
2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Bill Hiebert, Damian Penner and Michael Zwaagstra. Also present:
City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen, and City Clerk,
Deb Rempel.
3. Councillor Damian Penner opened the meeting.
R22-269 4. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.
-Un. Carried-
R22-270 5. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the minutes of the
November 15, 2022 Regular Council Meeting be approved.
-Un. Carried-
R22-271 6. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that Councillor J. Siemens,
Councillor S. Penner and Councillor J. Hiebert be excused from the meeting.
-Un. Carried-
7. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m., by Mayor
Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider By-Law 2166.
By-Law 2166
Road Closing and Road renaming Industrial Road West to Campbell Road.
Purpose: To close a portion of the road at the corner of Industrial Road West and Acres
Drive and to rename Industrial Road West to Campbell Road.
7.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager introduced By-Law 2166 and reported that notices pursuant to The Municipal Act had been completed.
7.2 There are no items of correspondence are on file.
7.3 There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.
7.4 There being no further discussion Council meeting re opened at 7:39 p.m.
R22-272 8. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give second reading to By-Law 2166, being a road closing and road renaming by-law.
-Un. Carried-
R22-273 9. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give third reading to By-Law 2166, being a road closing and road renaming by-law.
-Un. Carried-
