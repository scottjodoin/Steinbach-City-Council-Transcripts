CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
May 7, 2024
MINUTES
1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
May 7, 2024 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.
2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Michael Zwaagstra, Bill Hiebert,
Susan Penner & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen, and Tax & Revenue Manager, Kara Mantie.
3. Mayor Funk opened the meeting.
R24-091 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.
-Un. Carried-
R24-092 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the minutes of the
April 16, 2024, Regular Council Meeting be approved.
-Un. Carried-
6. Troy Warkentin, City Manager, reported that notice had been received from Manitoba
Transportation & Infrastructure advising they have planned a concrete joint repair program for
Main Street for this spring/summer with intention to start work, Monday, May 13, 2024. The duration of the project is unknown, but they did indicate that East & West bound lanes of Main
Street between Brandt Street and Kroeker Avenue would be affected. He anticipated appropriate notices would be provided to affected businesses/travelling public in due course.
6.1 Councillor S. Penner asked if there were any updates on repairs to PTH 12 North.
6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, provided there was no update on the repairs at this time however is aware that Manitoba Transportation & Infrastructure are considering an asphalt overlay program for Brandt Street & PTH 12 North. To what extent and when that may occur is currently unknown.
