CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
August 6, 2024

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
August 6, 2024 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Damian Penner,
Bill Hiebert, & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate
Services, Adam Thiessen, and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor M. Zwaagstra opened the meeting.

R24-154 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R24-155 5. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the minutes of the July 16,
2024, Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Council meeting recessed, and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2024-07.

Variance V-2024-07

Owner: [name withheld]

Applicant: G&E Homes

Civic Address: 101/103 Winston Place

Legal: Lot 27, Plan 68657

Purpose of Variance V-2024-07: To allow a 18.2 foot rear yard setback whereas the
“RMD” Residential Medium Density Zone requires a minimum rear yard setback of 25 feet to construct a covered back deck on the back of the dwelling of 103 Winston Place.

6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Variance V-2024-07, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

6.2 The applicant was present at the public hearing.

6.3 There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns

6.4 There being no further discussion Council meeting re-opened at 7:39 p.m.

R24-156 7. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2024-07.
