16. Councillor B. Hiebert requested clarification from administration regarding hedges protruding onto sidewalks and the course of action that can be taken.
16.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager stated that if a tree or shrub is located on city property, the homeowner notify administration, which will direct staff to take care of it. If a tree or shrub is located on private property and encroaching onto city property, it is the homeowner's responsibility to ensure trees and shrubs are trimmed appropriately. Administration also recommends inquires be directed to the City office for staff to follow up with the homeowner if specific addresses are identified.
