CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
April 2, 2024

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
April 2, 2024 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Bill Hiebert, Michael Zwaagstra &
Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services,
Adam Thiessen, and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

7. Council Meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m., by Mayor
Funk. Public hearing was to consider the City of Steinbach 2024 Financial Plan.

7.1 Brian Hrehirchuk, Senior Manager, Finance, presented the 2024 Financial Plan.

Mr. Hrehirchuk provided information on the annual budget cycle and that City Council,

Management and staff have been working on the budget for several months, with Council

providing comments at the final review in November.

7.2 Mr. Hrehirchuk presented the budget proposing a municipal property tax rate of 14.4

mills for 2024.

7.3 Mr. Hrehirchuk provided information on what to expect for 2024 Expenses Programs and

Projects and Debt that make up the 2024 budget:

- $46.1 million for new capital improvements:

- Events Centre Construction

- $16.5 million capital renewal program for the replacement of equipment and

infrastructure:

- Loewen Boulevard and PTH 12 North Intersection
- Hanover Street upgrade

7.4 Mr. Hrehirchuk provided that the City of Steinbach is in receipt of the Notice of

Tax requirements for the Hanover School Division in accordance with Section 187 of

The Public Schools Act, under Section 188 for 2024 the amount to be raised by tax levy

is $15,120,196.00.

7.5 Mr. Hrehirchuk provided that the City of Steinbach is in receipt of the Notice of

Tax requirements for the Manitoba Education and Training - 2024 Education Support

Levy in accordance with sections 182 and 183 of The Public Schools Act, 2024

Other Property to be raised by tax levy is $3,246,419.00.

7.6 Mr. Hrehirchuk stated that there was strong development in 2023 and property value growth of 4.4%. Supplementary property taxes of $675,000.00, a 4.6 million provincial per capita tax sharing grant and Federal Gas Tax Grant $890,500.00 are expected.

7.7 Mr. Hrehirchuk provided a long-term overview for expected requirements in future years.

7.8 Mr. Hrehirchuk completed his presentation at 7:46 p.m.

7.9 Mr. Hrehirchuk was prepared to answer questions of council.

7.10 There was no one present with questions or concerns at the meeting.

7.11 Mayor Funk thanked Mr. Hrehirchuk for a well-prepared presentation.

meeting re-opened 7:47 p.m., and the following resolutions resulted.

R24-073 8. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Hiebert
WHEREAS Section 162 of The Municipal Act, SM 1996, c.58 states:
“Every council must adopt a financial plan for each fiscal year in a form approved by the minister and consisting of (a) an operating budget; (b) a capital budget; (c) an estimate of operating revenue and expenditures for the following fiscal year; and (d) a five-year capital expenditure program.”
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the financial plan for the City of Steinbach for the year 2024 as set out in the manner and form approved by the Minister be and the same is hereby adopted, and that the said plan information shall be incorporated in and form part of the
2024 Tax Levy By-Law.

-Un. Carried-

11. Delegation: R.C.M.P. Staff Sergeant, Guy Landreville and Heidi Harder-Falk, Headway
Program was present at the meeting.

11.1 Staff Sergeant Landreville reviewed statistics from 2022 to 2023, highlighting the following:

- Traffic accidents and impaired infractions have shown an increase.

- Provincial traffic offenses such as speeding, street racing, noise have resulted in fines, suspensions and vehicle impoundment continues to be enforced. The dedicated traffic member is making an impact and suggest a second dedicated member would be even more effective. He confirmed that the numbers for 2024 will continue to increase from 2023.

--- Page 3 ---

- The traffic division continues to work out the detachment. He will send requests and they will attend to specific areas, but their main focus continues to be as previously outlined.

- Break and Enter and Mischief to Property has had a significant increase. Addiction to substances such as methamphetamine can drive individuals to commit crimes such as break and enters and theft to support their habits. The revolving door system often fails to address the root cause of these behaviors, resulting in individuals cycling in and out of the criminal justice system without receiving the help they need.

- The Headway Program provides support and resources to individuals aiming to break the cycle of addiction driven crime and helps these individuals rebuild their lives.

11.2 Staff Sergeant Landreville outlined and elaborated on the Council policing priorities and performance plan for 2023-2024 and proposed for 2024-2025.

a. Communication - Visibility of Police

b. Crime Reduction - Organized Crime

c. Crime Reduction - Property Crimes/Break and Enters/Vandalism

d. Crime Reduction - Traffic

e. Respectful Workplace

11.3 Mayor Funk thanked the Staff Sergeant for his report and for working together with council to provide a safe community.

11.4 Heidi Harder-Falk, Youth Co-Ordinator, Headway Program, located at the Steinbach
RCMP detachment at 100 PTH 12 North and provided the following:

- The youth program works with clients ages 12-19, with the adult restart program, led by
[name withheld], working with clients ages 18 and older for justice involved adults.

- They partner with the RCMP, Probation Services, Shared Health, (including the
Addictions Foundation), Southern Health (including Community Health), the Hanover School
Division and Child and Family Services along with other community services.

- They provide a support team who work with the client to deal with substance abuse and mental health issues. Clients are supported and held accountable.

- They have seen a 98% reduction in youth involvement with the justice system after being involved with the program.

- They also work within the community to take preventative or early intervention measures such as the Community Safety and Wellbeing Initiative, Steinbach Alternative Justice
Committee and parent support groups.

11.5 Mayor Funk thanked Ms. Harder-Falk for working together with the R.C.M.P. to provide support to the youth of the community.
