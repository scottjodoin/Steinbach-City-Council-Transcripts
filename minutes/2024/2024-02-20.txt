1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
February 20, 2024 at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

Council present: Councillors Damian Penner, Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Bill Hiebert, Jac

6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:36 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Public Reserve Closing By-Law 2235.

By-Law 2235

Owner: The City of Steinbach

Civic Address: Heritage Parkway

Legal: Public Reserve Plan 64165

Purpose of By-Law 2235: To close a portion of the public reserve to allow for the extension of Heritage Parkway.

6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced By-Law 2235, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided written correspondence had been received from
Manitoba Municipal Relations - Community Planning Branch regarding By-Law 2235 voicing no concerns.

7. There being no further discussion Council meeting re-opened at 7:38 p.m.

12. Delegation: Audrey Harder, Executive Director, Pat Porter Active Living Centre, was present at the meeting to request a letter of support from City Council for its plans to submit a bid to host the Manitoba Society of Seniors (MSOS) 55 Plus Games in 2025.

12.1 Ms. Harder explained that after being approached by Active Aging in Manitoba to place a bid to host the games in 2025, and in speaking with Russ Dyck, Manager, Parks and Recreation,
Dan Taillefer, Recreation Services Coordinator and other community stakeholders to determine if there was interest in bidding.

12.2 Ms. Harder has received an overwhelming response. It has been over 12 years since
Steinbach last hosted the games, and the Southeast Event Centre will be operational by 2025. It will be an amazing opportunity to showcase Steinbach to people throughout the province.

12.3 Ms. Harder stated that the purpose of the games is to provide a recreation outlet for friendly competition for those who are 55 years of age or older, to provide and promote opportunities and contribute to the education, culture and social activity and general wellbeing of
Manitobans 55 and older, as well as engender a sense of community spirit by providing an opportunity and catalyst for bringing older adults, their families and the community at large together to participate in the games.

12.4 The proposed dates for the event are June 3-5, 2025. They have requested to host earlier in June as Summer in the City will be held later in the month.

12.5 Ms. Harder listed the Southeast Event Center, Pat Porter Active Living Center, Steinbach
Bowling Alley and Bistro, Aquatic Centre, Fly-In Golf Course and Parks and Rec. all being used for the event, while bringing over 1000 people to Steinbach.

12.6 Ms. Harder requested a letter of Support from the city to submit with their application, and to consider assisting financially if Steinbach was selected to host in 2025. She was prepared to answer questions of council.

13. The Mayor thanked the delegate for their presentation and the work that Ms. Harder does.

R24-039 14. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach fully support Ms. Harder and the Pat Porter Active Living Centre in its bid to host the Manitoba
Society of Seniors (MSOS) 55 Plus Provincial Games in Steinbach in June 2025.

-Un. Carried-

15. Delegation: [Resident 1] was present at the meeting to request rapid flashing low level pedestrian crossing lights be installed at the crosswalks on Main Street by Quality Detailing and
Jodoin Law Office and beside the 7-11 and Bowling Alley, across Brandt Street.

15.1 [Resident 1] explained that his purpose in coming before council was to save lives. He cited a situation on December 15th where he witnessed three children who were almost hit as they were crossing, a young child who was hit on Main Street three years ago, as well as an 8- year-old child who did lose his life three years ago while crossing at St. Anne’s Road in
Winnipeg.

15.2 [Resident 1] has previously spoken to members of council by email and phone. Three years ago, he contacted MTI with concerns. They stated they would conduct a study, but he has not heard back from them. He explained that the City of Winnipeg, the town of Ste. Anne and other municipalities have installed rapid flashing low level pedestrian crossing lights, installed at the seven-foot height.

15.3 [Resident 1] was prepared to answer questions of council.

16. The Mayor thanked the delegate for their presentation.
