14. Adam Thiessen, Manager, Corporate Services presented an Event Centre Update.

14.1 Highlighted the installation of steel structure for the arena bowl area, final roof truss, to be completed by the end of April. Change rooms and showers continue to take shape, with plumbing and electrical being installed.

14.2 Ice plant condenser is on site, with the old ice plant being decommissioned.

14.3 Pre-cast concrete for tiered seating and hollow core flooring is approximately 85% complete.

14.4 Exterior insulation and weather proofing is in progress. Metal sheeting is expected to begin in the next couple of months.

14.5 Progress report reviewed, with 46.5% of total project cost to date.

15. Mr. Thiessen was prepared to answer questions of council.

16. Troy Warkentin, City Manager presented the Traffic Count Report focusing on the corner of Loewen Boulevard and Hespeler Street intersection.

--- Page 3 ---

16.1 Traffic flows and level of service at the intersection were well understood. Based on
WSP’s assessment of the intersection, no improvements were considered required given northbound Hespeler’s LOS of E. However, the results show that it would improve the overall
LOS for the intersection during the PM peak hour scenario, at the cost of longer delays for the
Loewen Boulevard west bound and east bound traffic during the PM peak hour. This would also be expected at all other times of the day since it would no longer be free-flowing.

16.2 Recommendation that more data be collected to gain a full understanding of the cause of the delays before making changes to the intersection, since an all-way stop-controlled intersection could result in unintended consequences for Loewen Boulevard traffic.

17. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that a three way stop sign be installed at the intersection of Loewen Boulevard and Hespeler Street.

-Motion Defeated-

In Favor: J. Siemens, J. Hiebert, Bill Hiebert
Against: M. Zwaagstra, D. Penner, S. Penner, E. Funk
