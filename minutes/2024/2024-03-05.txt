10.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Variance V-2024-01, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

R24-053 11. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2024-01 subject to the execution of a development agreement.

-Un. Carried-

12. Troy Warkentin, City Manager, reported that the City has received The 2023 Steinbach
Annual Traffic Count Program, as submitted by WSP Canada Inc. The traffic count and analysis program were conducted at 21 intersections in Steinbach along PTH 12 North & Brandt Street and PTH 52 West & Main Street. The traffic counts were conducted on Thursday, October 19,
Friday October 20, Thursday October 26 and Friday, October 27 during the weekday peak period from 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m

12.1 Mr. Warkentin Highlighted the following sections:

1) Traffic volume trends

2) Traffic signal warrant analysis

3) Recommendations

- Recommendations for Unsignalized Intersections

- Signals are warranted at the intersection of PTH 52 West and Giesbrecht
Street. Signalization work should be completed by Manitoba Transportation and Infrastructure in the 2024 construction season.

- Recommendations for Signalized Intersections

- PTH 12 North at Superstore Access/Co-op Access intersection be subject to improvements, which the City would continue to monitor.

12. 2 The Steinbach Annual Traffic Count Program was acknowledged by City Council and discussion and resolution followed.

R24-054 13. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner
BE IT RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach accept the 2023 Steinbach Annual Traffic
Count Program as presented.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach forward a copy of the
Steinbach Annual Traffic Count Program report (dated February 13, 2024) to Manitoba
Transportation and Infrastructure;

-Un. Carried

14. Delegation: Michelle Bezditny, Director of Economic Development, and Christine
Beaumont were present at the meeting to provide an update on the Steinbach Economic
Development Corporation.

14.1 Ms. Beaumont spoke to the Steinbach Economic Developments continued vison and explained one of the reasons they became incorporated was to be eligible for funding not accessible by the Chamber of Commerce.

14.2 Ms. Bezditny identified the four key priorities of economic development, defined agribusiness and explained why it was a focus for the SEDC for Steinbach. Ms. Bezditny gave a detailed description of the advanced agribusiness ecosystem.

14.3 Ms. Bezditny presented the 2023 outcomes, including the Can Export grant funding they have received from Global Affairs Canada. She then reviewed the Rural Manitoba Economic
Development Corporation Municipal Investment Readiness investment factors, both for what the
City requires and what potential investors are seeking.

14.4 Ms. Beaumont explained that the newly elected board has completed a strategic planning session to determine short and long-term goals. They will proceed with creating an operational plan. She stated that one of the active projects for 2024 was to complete a business audit for existing businesses. The delegation was prepared to answer questions of council.

15. The Deputy Mayor thanked the delegates for their presentation.
