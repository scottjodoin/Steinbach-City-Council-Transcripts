CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
September 3, 2024

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
September 3, 2024, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Damian Penner &
Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Mayor Earl Funk opened the meeting.

R24-171 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R24-172 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the minutes of the
August 20, 2024, Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

R24-173 6. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that Councillor Bill Hiebert be excused from the meeting.

-Un. Carried-

7. Angele Cuthbertson, Donor Relations & Development Officer, on behalf of S.T.A.R.S. was present at the meeting.

- Ms. Cuthbertson provided that the 2023/2024 year saw 1,153 missions flown in
Manitoba, with 38 directly to the City of Steinbach and 41 to the area within 50 kms of
Steinbach.

- Steinbach’s participation allows S.T.A.R.S to invest in advanced training for flight and medical crews, equipping them with the latest skills and knowledge to handle complex medical situations.

- Video shown outlining where donations are allotted.

7.1 The Mayor thanked the delegate for the presentation and for the work that S.T.A.R.S. provides the community and surrounding area.
