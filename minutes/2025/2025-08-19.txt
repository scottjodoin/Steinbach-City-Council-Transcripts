CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
August 19, 2025

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
August 19, 2025, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Damian Penner, Michael Zwaagstra,
Bill Hiebert & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin and City Clerk,
Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Jake Hiebert opened the meeting.

R25-159 4. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.

-Un. Carried-

R25-160 5. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the minutes of August 5,
2025, Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Troy Warkentin, City Manager, presented an update on the Manitoba Water Services
Board grant.

6.1 The City has received confirmation of grant funding in the amount of $3,650,000.00 for the Forcemain Twinning project, which includes upgrades to Lift Station No. 1. This project has been identified in the City’s long-term capital plan since 2021. Council previously passed a resolution to apply for this funding, and no further action is required at this time.

14. Correspondence and a request from TobaGrown to enact a resolution to hold a Cannabis
Retail plebiscite in conjunction with the upcoming 2026 Election were acknowledged.

R25-167 15. M. Zwaagstra, J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach not permit the initiation of a plebiscite by resolution respecting cannabis sales in the City of Steinbach at the time of the next municipal election being held October 28, 2026.

Voting For: M. Zwaagstra, J. Hiebert, E. Funk,
S. Penner, D. Penner & B. Hiebert
Voting Against: J. Siemens
