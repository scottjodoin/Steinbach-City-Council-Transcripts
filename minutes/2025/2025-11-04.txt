CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
November 4, 2025

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
November 4, 2025, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Damian Penner, Michael Zwaagstra,
Bill Hiebert & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Senior Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Bill Hiebert opened the meeting.

R25-207 4. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted, as amended:

-Un. Carried-

6. Adam Thiessen, Senior Manager, Corporate Services, presented an update on the contracted security program.

- At the public meeting held on June 17, Council authorized administration to explore and procure contracted security services to enhance monitoring and deter criminal activity within the
City.

- Steinbach Security Services was subsequently contracted to deliver services on a pilot project basis, operating month-to-month beginning July 2 and continuing to November 25, 2025.

- Funding for this initiative was drawn from the unspent portion of the RCMP budget allocation. The estimated budget required to continue the program in 2026 is $145,000.

- Feedback from the RCMP and several local developers has been positive, with reports of reduced theft and vandalism, and the RCMP noting an increased ability to focus on higher-priority matters.

- Administration recommends the continuation of the Overnight Security Patrol Pilot
Program for the remainder of 2025. For 2026 and subsequent years, it is recommended that the program be reviewed as part of the City’s annual budget discussions and continued until such time that RCMP staffing levels adequately address overnight property crime concerns such as theft and vandalism.

6.1 Mr. Thiessen was prepared to answer questions of council.

R25-209 7. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor S. Penner RESOLVED that the overnight security patrol program be continued to the end of 2025, and that it be included in the 2026 budget for consideration by council.

-Un. Carried-

8. Adam Thiessen, Senior Manager, Corporate Services, presented the Lift Station 1 generator request for approval.

- Generator replacement is part of the planned 2026 project to upgrade Lift Station No. 1 and twin the force main from Lift Station No. 1 to the lagoon.

- The existing generator will be upgraded to a 1,500-kilowatt unit. Due to extended equipment lead times, which exceed the typical capital project tender schedule.

- Administration requests council’s approval to procure the associated engineering services from Toromont CAT at a cost of $670,600.00 plus applicable taxes, to ensure installation aligns with construction progress scheduled for next summer.
