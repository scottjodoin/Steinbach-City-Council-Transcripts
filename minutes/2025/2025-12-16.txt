12. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:49 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Conditional Use CU-2025-05.

Conditional Use CU-2025-05

Owner: Neustaedter Holdings Inc. & [name withheld]

Applicant: Waldo Neustaedter

Civic Address: 190, 198, 204 & 206 First Street

Legal: Lot 2, 3, 6 & 7, Block 1, Plan 10121

Purpose: To allow a multi-family residential development within the “C1” Commercial
Neighbourhood zone.

12.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager introduced Conditional Use CU-2025-05 and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

12.2 The applicant, Waldo Neustaedter, 32 Brandt Street, was present at the public hearing and presented the following information:
- With the location of the property, applying for the variances and conditional use will allow the project to keep the neighbourhood feeling.
- Removing the commercial component of the buildings will create an excellent buffer between the commercial district to the east and the residential community to the west.
- One proposed building will be three stories, and the other proposed building will be five stories, with the building having main floor heated indoor parking and four stories of residential.
- Both buildings will have a neighbourhood friendly curb appeal, taking advantage of the towering pine trees on the lot. They will be designed in a style which could be considered adult condo lifestyle without the cost of ownership or condo fees, and will include areas of green space.

--- Page 3 ---

- There are adequate parking stalls on site, and he has spoken to Lacey Gaudet, City
Planner, and Aaron Rach, City Engineer to discuss required siteworks, drainage system and sewage capacity.
- Mr. Neustaedter provided three letters of support for the project from the owners of 222
First Street, 61 Brandt Street and 195 First Street, stating they are aware of the nature of the project and do not oppose or dispute the construction.

12.3 [Resident 1], 185 First Street, was present at the public hearing voicing questions and concerns including:
- if the property is treated as a residential property for property tax purposes
- had a traffic impact study be done as there is a potential traffic flow impact as William
Avenue is a glorified single lane in poor condition.
- the section of First Street is not in good condition.
- would sewer and water upgrades and size of the development and number of units added.
Water has been upgraded on occasion when there have been breakdowns, and to his knowledge sewer has never been upgraded. Sewage usage could be double with infrastructure dating back to the 1960’s.

12.4 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, confirmed there is a plan to upgrade the underground services along First Street, however there is currently no specific date. It is on the city’s long-term capital plan to be completed within the next five years and will be a renewal project similar to what was previously completed on Kroeker Avenue. This would be contingent on timing and funding available. There was no traffic impact study required for this application. The city’s infrastructure system which presently exists is adequate to service the proposed development.

12.5 [Resident 1], 185 First Street, returned to the podium and inquired whether the city would consider making William a one way to facilitate truck traffic from Main to First Street.

12.6 Mr. Warkentin clarified that First Street is designated as a collector street under the city’s
Official Community Plan and collector streets provide connections between local streets and the arterial street system. Private access to collector streets should be restricted where possible.
Two-lane collector streets like First Street are generally designed to carry traffic volumes between
1,000 & 4,000 vehicles per day.

12.7 [Resident 2], 226 First Street was present at the public hearing with concerns of parking impacts and stormwater coming off the site as First Street tends to flood.

13. There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 8:09 p.m.

R25-248 14. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor M. Zwaagstra RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Conditional Use CU-2025-05, subject to the execution of a development agreement.

-Un. Carried-

Variance V-2025-35

Owner: Neustaedter Holdings Inc. & [name withheld]

Applicant: Waldo Neustaedter
