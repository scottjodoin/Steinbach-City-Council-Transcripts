CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
January 21, 2025

MINUTES

1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
January 21, 2025, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Bill Hiebert,
Damian Penner & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen, and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

3. Councillor Jake Hiebert opened the meeting.

R25-010 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted, as amended.

Remove: Item 9H Designate Tax Sale Year - 2020 (resolution)
Remove: Item 9I Appoint Tax Sale Manager (resolution)

-Un. Carried-

R25-011 5. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor J. Siemens, RESOLVED that the minutes of the
January 7, 2025, Regular Council Meeting be approved.

-Un. Carried-

6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:36 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Subdivision 4451-2024-9041.

Subdivision 4451-2024-9041

Owner: Steinbach Community Development Corporation

Applicant: City of Steinbach

Civic Address: August Cove

Legal: Lot 3 Plan 44712

Purpose of Subdivision 4451-2024-9041: To allow the creation of 11 new industrial lots, as well as the opening of August Cove, being a public road.

6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Subdivision 4451-2024-9041, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided written correspondence had been received from
Manitoba Municipal Relations - Community Planning Branch regarding 4451-2024-9041 voicing no concerns.

6.3 There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.

6.4 There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:38 p.m.

R25-012 7. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor M. Zwaagstra

RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve application for subdivision 4451-2024-
9041 as applied for by the City of Steinbach, on behalf of the owner, Steinbach Community
Development Corporation;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve the opening of August
Cove, being a public road.

-Un. Carried-
