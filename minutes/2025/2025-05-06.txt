9.3 [Resident 1] of 444 Hespeler Street South, the Applicant was present at the public hearing and provided they are seeking a variance for the site width. Requesting a four foot side yard setback on the corner would enhance the functionality of the site, allowing for slightly large suites that would benefit future occupants. He noted that the sidewalk is located on the opposite side of the street, creating no issues related to traffic visibility. While the variance would improve the functionality of the development, a site plan has already been approved by administration which works without the variance. Mr. Loewen was prepared to answer questions of council.
10. Council discussion and questions followed.
10.1 [Resident 2], 287 Henry Street, was present at the public hearing to express concerns regarding the maintenance and upkeep of the property. He noted that since the current owners took possession, the lawn has been allowed to grow 18 inches high and is overrun with weeds and garbage. [Resident 2] questioned whether the owners would properly maintain the property in the future.
10.2 Mr. Loewen returned to the podium and explained that the current building has exceeded its lifespan and has attracted tenants who have not maintained the property. He was confident redevelopment would encourage better care of the property.
10.3 [Resident 2] returned to the podium and stated that, to his understanding, it is the responsibility of the property owner to maintain it.
10.4 [Resident 3], owner of the property, agreed the existing house is an eyesore and has attracted the wrong tenants. He confirmed that snow removal and yard maintenance service would be contracted out.
11. There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:48 p.m.
R25-093 12. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve the overall site width of 64.5 feet and deny the corner side yard setback of 4 feet for
Variance V-2025-07, subject to rezoning approval.

15.1 Matt Bergen, #2 George Place, provided information on the floodlight campaign at AD
Penner Park explained that the Raiders were founded in 1996, empowering youth ages 4-18 by providing them with an opportunity to play football, fostering activity, teamwork and a sense of community. Each year they see approximately 150 youths throughout various levels. They also offer flag clinics to local schools during the off season.
Growing numbers at the club are creating scheduling challenges. Installing lights would ease scheduling pressures. Current practices are cut short due to the reduced daylight hours in the fall. This would extend practice hours, improving visibility and safety.
In addition to improving the infrastructure at AD Penner Park, approximately 400-500 visitors travel to Steinbach on a gameday weekend, bringing additional business to local hotels and restaurants.
Mr. Bergen provided a brief summary of project costs and estimates provided by local manufactures and installers and a request of $71,000.00 from the City of Steinbach.

28. Councillor M. Zwaagstra reported that both he and Mayor Funk serve on the RCMP
Advisory Committee. He emphasized that traffic enforcement remains a priority within the City and noted an increase in public reports regarding street racing on Main Street, with a corresponding desire for enhanced enforcement.
At the most recent advisory committee meeting, the Staff Sergeant was encouraged to explore all available options to support traffic enforcement efforts. Councillor Zwaagstra noted that the RCMP are often operating with a shortfall of 2 to 4 officers, which impacts their ability to carry out adequate enforcement activities.
Staff Sergeant Ryll has proposed a collaborative enforcement initiative with the Town of
Ste. Anne. They are willing to pursue independent enforcement measures funded through
Manitoba Public Insurance, which would allow for increased traffic enforcement within the City.
29. Consensus of council was to collaborate with the town of Ste. Anne for additional enforcement of traffic, as proposed by the Steinbach RCMP detachment and to be funded by
Manitoba Public Insurance.
