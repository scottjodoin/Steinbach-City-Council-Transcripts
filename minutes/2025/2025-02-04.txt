CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
February 4, 2025
MINUTES
1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
February 4, 2025, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.
2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Michael Zwaagstra, Bill Hiebert,
Damian Penner & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen, and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.
3. Councillor Damian Penner opened the meeting.
R25-023 4. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.
-Un. Carried-
R25-024 5. Councillor J. Hiebert, Councillor D. Penner, RESOLVED that the minutes of
January 21, 2025, Regular Council Meeting be approved.
-Un. Carried-
6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:36 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider By-Law 2255 - Local Improvement Plan
2025-01; Lift Station #1 to Lagoon Forcemain Twinning.
By-Law 2255
Local Improvement Plan 2025-01
Lift Station #1 to Lagoon Forcemain Twinning
Purpose: To install 3050 meters of new force main along Park Road West between Lift
Station #1 and the Lagoon; Lift Station #1 upgrade.
6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced By-Law 2255, Local Improvement Plan
2025-01, and reported that notices pursuant to The Municipal Act had been completed.
6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided no written correspondence on file.
6.3 There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.
6.4 There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:38 p.m.
R25-025 7. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give first reading to By-Law 2255, Local Improvement Plan 2025-01, Lift Station #1 to Lagoon
Forcemain Twinning.
-Un. Carried-
R25-026 8. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give second reading to By-Law 2255, Local Improvement Plan 2025-01, Lift Station #1 to Lagoon
Forcemain Twinning.
-Un. Carried-
9. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:41 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider By-Law 2257 - Special Service Plan 2025-01;
Residential Curbside Waste Collection.
By-Law 2257
Special Service Plan 2025-01
Residential Curbside Waste Collection
Purpose: To approve amendments to the rates charged for curbside collection and disposal of solid waste for residential properties.

12. Troy Warkentin, City Manager, provided an Event Centre Update.
12.1 The parking lot and exterior of the building are nearing completion, with minor areas such as the exit stairs on Hanover Street, and exterior signage still outstanding.
12.2 The Locker rooms and rest rooms have fixtures installed and are close to completion.
Public washrooms, the Club Zone and suites are currently in progress. The general concourse and Piston’s gear shop are taking shape with the stairway in the atrium nearing completion.
12.3 Arena bowl media level 300 interior finishing being done. The arena bowl ice surface lighting is operational. The cool-down ice floor process will start on February 10th and will take approximately one week. Creating the ice surface is planned for February 17th.
12.4 Progress report reviewed to December 31, 2024.
13. Mr. Warkentin was prepared to answer questions of council.
