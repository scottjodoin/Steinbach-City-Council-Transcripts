CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
February 18, 2025
MINUTES
1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
February 18, 2025, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.
2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Michael Zwaagstra, Bill Hiebert, Damian Penner &
Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate Services, Adam
Thiessen, and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.
3. Councillor Jac Siemens opened the meeting.
R25-032 4. Councillor D. Penner, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.
-Un. Carried-
R25-033 5. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor J. Siemens, RESOLVED that the minutes of February
4, 2025, Regular Council Meeting be approved.
-Un. Carried-
6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:39 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Conditional Use CU-2025-01.
Conditional Use CU-2025-01
Owner: Solomon Budala Holdings Inc.
Applicant: Avedo Inc.
Civic Address: 86 Industrial Road
Legal: Lot 2, Plan 69051
Purpose: To allow the operation of a retail sales business on the subject property. As per
Zoning By-Law 2100, retail sales is a conditional use in the “M1” Light Industrial Zone.
6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Conditional Use CU-2025-01, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.
6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided no written correspondence on file.
6.3 Stefan Hodelmann, 36161 Loewen Boulevard, on behalf of Avedo Inc. was present at the council meeting and provided the following:
- The proposal does not seek retail sales permission for the entire site, only for the specific tenant, Adult and Teen Challenge.- The thrift store is a vehicle to effectively achieve the goals of Adult and Teen challenges model.
- Currently operating at 354 Main Street, Adult and Teen Challenge provides counseling services and office space. However, their current location has become inadequate due to parking constraints, space limitations, and structural issues caused by neighboring redevelopment.
- The thrift store is intended as a means to support Adult and Teen Challenge’s mission.
Their model includes cleaning, assembling, and refurbishing goods for sale in a retail space.
Employment opportunities will be available for both full-time staff and individuals participating in the program.
6.4 Mr. Hodelmann was prepared to answer questions of council.
6.5 Daniel Emond, 510 Pepperloaf Crescent, Winnipeg, of Adult and Teen Challenge was present and provided the following:
- They were unable to find a 10,000-square-foot commercial space that would allow them to efficiently operate their program. As an alternative, [name withheld] proposed constructing a facility to meet their needs and provide financial sustainability.

--- Page 2 ---

- Mr. Emond emphasized the importance of the thrift store component of their program, noting that they serve as a safe environment where clients and students engage in work therapy.
Additionally, revenue from store sales helps fund the organization’s programs. He expressed concern that closing the thrift store and not permitting them to relocate to 86 Industrial Road would be a significant setback, as it also serves as a venue for support groups and addiction counseling.
6.6 Mr. Hodelmann returned to the podium to inform council that due to the development next door to 354 Main Street they will be required to move out before next winter and are working under a time crunch.
6.7 There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 8:03 p.m.
R25-034 7. Councillor J. Siemens, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach deny
Conditional Use CU-2025-01.
-Un. Carried-
8. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 8:12 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider rezoning By-Law 2260.
By-Law 2260 - Rezoning
Owner: JDL Holdings Ltd.
Applicant: Vogt Building Construction
Civic Address: 79 & 85 First Street
Legal: Lot 2, Block 1, Plan 34253
& Lot 22, Plan 4068 Exc. Lane 5507
Purpose: To rezone the properties from “RLD” Residential Low Density to
“RMD” Residential Medium Density to allow for a multi-family development to be constructed.
8.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced By-Law 2260, and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.
8.2 Written correspondence has been received from Manitoba Municipal Relations -
Community Planning Branch regarding By-Law 2260 voicing no concerns.
8.3 Jared Vogt, 84 Lake Park Drive, Winnipeg, was present at the hearing and was prepared to answer questions of council.
8.4 There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 8:15 p.m.
R25-035 9. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give second reading to By-Law 2260, subject to the execution of a Development Agreement.
-Un. Carried-
10. Troy Warkentin, City Manager, provided a report on Harmony Lane at Cutlass Drive
Stop-Control.
10.1 The report was prepared at the request of the council on January 21st. The intersection at
Harmony Lane and Cutlass Drive was analyzed. The City Engineer conducted the review and prepared the report.
10.2 Research included the review of pedestrian and vehicle traffic flows at the intersection as well as the consideration of other site specific factors.
10.3 Based on the findings of the review, the signage that is present at the intersection and the stop sign that is already there, no changes are warranted. Concerns regarding traffic disobeying the posted speed limit should be considered an enforcement issue and dealt with by the R.C.M.P.
10.4 Recommendation of administration is that no changes to signage at the intersection are required and no changes be made.
11. Mr. Warkentin was prepared to answer questions of council.
