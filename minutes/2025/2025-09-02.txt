CITY OF STEINBACH
Regular Council Meeting
September 2, 2025
MINUTES
1. Minutes of the Regular Council Meeting of City of Steinbach Council held on Tuesday,
September 2, 2025, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.
2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Damian Penner, Michael Zwaagstra,
Bill Hiebert & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Manager, Corporate
Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.
3. Councillor Damian Penner opened the meeting.
R25-168 4. Councillor B. Hiebert, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the agenda be adopted.
-Un. Carried-
R25-169 5. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor D. Penner RESOLVED that the minutes of August 19,
2025, Regular Council Meeting be approved.
-Un. Carried-
6. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:35 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2025-20.
Variance V-2025-20
Owner: [names withheld]
Applicant: [name withheld]
Civic Address: 22 Country View Lane
Legal: Lot 1, Plan 49241
Purpose: To permit an overall site width of 62 feet whereas the “RR” Rural Residential
Zone requires a minimum of 100 feet.
6.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager, introduced Variance V-2025-20, reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.
6.2 Troy Warkentin, City Manager provided no written correspondence on file
6.3 The Owner was present and willing to answer questions of council.
6.4 There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.
7. There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:37 p.m.
R25-170 8. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor J. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2025-20.
-Un. Carried-
R25-171 9. Councillor M. Zwaagstra, Councillor S. Penner RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approve Variance V-2025-14.
-Un. Carried-
10. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:40 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Conditional Use CU-2025-04.
