November 18, 2025, at City of Steinbach Council Chambers.

2. Mayor Earl Funk called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m., with the following members of
Council present: Councillors Jake Hiebert, Susan Penner, Damian Penner, Michael Zwaagstra,
Bill Hiebert & Jac Siemens. Also present: City Manager, Troy Warkentin, Senior Manager,
Corporate Services, Adam Thiessen and City Clerk, Amanda Dubois.

7. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:40 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Rezoning By-Law 2266.

By-Law 2266 - Rezoning

Owner: [name withheld]
Applicant: [name withheld]

Civic Address: 635 & 641 Main Street

Legal: Lot 15, Block 3, Plan 6754 Excl SE 465 feet and

Lot 16, Block 3, Plan 6754 Excl NW 10 feet

Purpose: To rezone the properties from “RLD”: Residential Low Density to “RMD”
Residential Medium Density to allow for a multi-family development to be constructed.

7.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager introduced By-Law 2266 and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

7.2 No items of correspondence had been received objecting to the application.

7.3 Terry Thiessen, 72 Norwood Bay Mitchell, of Hillside Custom, on behalf of the applicants, was present at the hearing and provided that the proposed multi-family development would be a good fit for the neighbourhood and fit the property the best.

7.4 Mr. Thiessen was prepared to answer questions of council.

8. Council discussion and questions followed.

9. [Resident 1] of 630 Wilson Street was present at the public hearing to express concerns regarding lighting, requesting that it be directed downward onto the property rather than across, as well as concerns about the number of dogs that could reside behind her property.

10. Mr. Thiessen returned to the podium to confirm that the parking lot lighting would be installed on the building itself and directed downward toward the parking lot. He noted that he could not comment on the number of dogs permitted, as that decision does not rest with the builder.

11. There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 7:43 p.m.

R25-221
12. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor B. Hiebert RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach give second reading to By-Law 2266, subject to the execution of a development agreement.

-Un. Carried-

13. Council meeting recessed and Public Hearing was called to order at 7:45 p.m., by
Mayor Earl Funk. Public Hearing was to consider Variance V-2025-32.

Variance V-2025-32

Owner: [name withheld]
Applicant: [name withheld]

Civic Address: 635 & 641 Main Street

Legal: Lot 15, Block 3, Plan 6754 Excl SE 465 feet and

Lot 16, Block 3, Plan 6754 Excl NW 10 feet

Purpose: To permit 45 parking stalls whereas 59 parking stalls are required for the proposed development.

13.1 Troy Warkentin, City Manager introduced Variance V-2025-32 and reported that notices pursuant to The Planning Act had been completed.

13.2 Terry Thiessen, 72 Norwood Bay Mitchell, of Hillside Custom, on behalf of the applicants, was present at the hearing and provided the following:

-
The developer believes they can enhance the neighbourhood by constructing a new building on the property.
-
Smaller one-bedroom units are the most appropriate approach, and they prefer to keep the project to a two-storey building. The design was inspired by new studio-style developments in
Winnipeg.
-
They are targeting an $800 price point and the specific demographic of single professionals, describing it as a needed niche in the community. Given the property’s location on Main Street, they felt a higher-density development would be appropriate.
-
Parking presented challenges. They have worked with the City Planner and were left with a variance request.
-
It will be studio units throughout the entire building, with one-bedroom units under 400 square feet, suitable for a single occupant rather than families or roommates. Each unit would have one parking stall, allowing for 1.25 stalls per unit overall, which includes 25% visitor parking.

13.3 Mr. Thiessen was prepared to answer questions of council.

13.4 Council discussion and questions followed.

14. [Resident 2] of 630 Wilson Street was present at the public hearing and stated that his greatest concern was the size and orientation of the building and is quite happy that it will be two storeys, with a flat roof and the orientation of the parking lot.

15. There was no one present at the public hearing with questions or concerns.

16. There being no further discussion, Council meeting re-opened at 8:14 p.m.

R25-222 17. Councillor S. Penner, Councillor J. Siemens RESOLVED that the City of Steinbach approved Variance V-2025-32, subject to Rezoning By-Law 2266 receiving third reading.

Voting For: S. Penner, J. Siemens,
D. Penner, E. Funk & B. Hiebert
Voting Against: J. Hiebert, M. Zwaagstra
