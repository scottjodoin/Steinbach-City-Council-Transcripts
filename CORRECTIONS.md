# Reporting an error

If a word, a speaker name or anything else looks wrong, please tell us. Email
**steinbachcitycouncilreporting@gmail.com** (or open an issue on this repository). It helps to include:

- the meeting date (or `id`) and the time in the video, e.g. `2026-10-06 at 00:49:48`;
- what the transcript says;
- what was really said, or who really said it.

We check every report against the video and the minutes, correct the speaker map or the text, rebuild the
transcript, and list the correction in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). We use your email address only to reply and
never publish it.

**Privacy requests.** If you spoke at a meeting or are mentioned in one and your name still appears in a
transcript here, email us and we will remove it.
